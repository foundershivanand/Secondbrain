# Brain Rot App - Gamified Screen Time Blocker

## Core Idea

A gamified screen-time and app-blocking tool that uses a decaying brain avatar to visually disincentivize doomscrolling in real-time.

## Key Information

- The app is called 'Brain Rot'.
- It features a brain avatar that visually decays the more the user scrolls.
- Users can block specific 'brainrot' apps like TikTok, Instagram, and Snapchat.
- It includes gamification elements such as unlocking rewards and customizing the app.
- The marketing claims the app has been featured in The New York Times.

## Atomic Knowledge

No atomic knowledge blocks extracted.

## Insights

- Using negative visual reinforcement (a decaying avatar) flips the standard positive reinforcement loop of gamification to break addictive habits.
- The success of digital wellness apps relies on making the friction of doomscrolling highly visible and emotionally resonant.
