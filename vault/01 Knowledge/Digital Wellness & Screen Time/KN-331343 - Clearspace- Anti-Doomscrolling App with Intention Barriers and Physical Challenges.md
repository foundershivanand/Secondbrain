# Clearspace: Anti-Doomscrolling App with Intention Barriers and Physical Challenges

## Core Idea

Clearspace reduces compulsive phone use by introducing high-friction barriers—such as requiring users to state an intention, set a time limit, or complete physical exercises—before unlocking addictive apps.

## Key Information

- Clearspace requires users to set an intention and a time limit before every app session ('No intention, no entry').
- It features physical challenges, such as doing 10 pushups, to unlock blocked apps like Instagram.
- The app includes various challenge settings including pushups, squats, jumping rope, and deep breathing.
- Clearspace is backed by Y Combinator and was featured as a Product of the Day on Product Hunt.

## Insights

- Micro-barriers (like doing 10 pushups) exploit the gap between impulse and action, allowing conscious decision-making to override subconscious habits.
- Pairing digital vices with physical exercises gamifies self-regulation and turns a negative habit (scrolling) into a trigger for a positive habit (exercise).

## Actions

- [ ] Explore Clearspace or similar friction-based app blockers to curb mindless social media usage.
- [ ] Implement a manual 'friction rule' for digital habits: perform a physical movement (e.g., 5 deep breaths or 10 squats) before opening distracting apps.
