# Pallavi Anupallavi (1983) - Mani Ratnam's Directorial Debut

## Core Idea

Pallavi Anupallavi (1983) is a landmark Kannada romantic drama marking the directorial debut of Mani Ratnam and the leading role debut of Anil Kapoor, exploring unconventional mature relationships with a legendary soundtrack by Ilaiyaraaja.

## Key Information

- Directorial debut of acclaimed filmmaker Mani Ratnam.
- Anil Kapoor's debut in a leading role, which remains his only Kannada film.
- Co-stars veteran actress Lakshmi in a pivotal role.
- Features a highly acclaimed soundtrack composed by Ilaiyaraaja.
- Explores unconventional themes of love, specifically focusing on an age-gap relationship and emotional bonding beyond societal norms.
