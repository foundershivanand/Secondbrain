# Touch Grass App: Physical-World Friction for Digital Detox

## Core Idea

An anti-doomscrolling app that blocks access to other applications until the user physically goes outside and takes a photo of real grass.

## Key Information

- The app is called 'Touch Grass'.
- It acts as an app blocker that requires real-world physical verification (photographing grass) to unlock.
- It is designed to combat doomscrolling by introducing high-friction, outdoor physical actions.
- Curated by content creator iampascio as part of a list of apps that make doomscrolling too annoying to continue.

- Digital detox tools are shifting from passive timers to active, physical-world verification tasks (Proof of Physicality) to break cognitive loops.
- Gamifying or absurd-ifying digital limits (e.g., forcing someone to touch grass) leverages humor and physical movement to disrupt compulsive phone checking.
