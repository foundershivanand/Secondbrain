# "A" (Kannada Movie) - Upendra

## Core Idea

A landmark 1998 Kannada psychological drama/thriller film directed by and starring Upendra, renowned for its highly non-linear screenplay, meta-commentary on the film industry, and cult status.

## Key Information

- Directed by and starring Upendra in the lead role.
- Co-stars Chandini and Archana.
- Features a highly successful soundtrack composed by Gurukiran, which launched his career.
- Known for its experimental narrative structure and psychological themes.
