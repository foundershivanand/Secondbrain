# Pallavi Anupallavi (1983) - Mani Ratnam Directorial Debut

## Core Idea

Mani Ratnam's 1983 Kannada directorial debut, 'Pallavi Anupallavi', explores unconventional romantic dynamics, marked by early collaborations with Ilaiyaraaja and Balu Mahendra, and starring Anil Kapoor in a pivotal early career performance.

## Key Information

- 'Pallavi Anupallavi' is the 1983 directorial debut of acclaimed Indian filmmaker Mani Ratnam.
- The film marks Bollywood actor Anil Kapoor's debut in a leading role in South Indian cinema, starring alongside veteran actress Lakshmi and Kiran Vairale.
- Features cinematography by Balu Mahendra and a celebrated soundtrack composed by Ilaiyaraaja.
- The plot addresses mature and progressive themes around platonic vs. romantic bonds, infidelity, and societal perceptions of relationships with older partners.
