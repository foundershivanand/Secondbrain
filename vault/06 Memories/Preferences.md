# ⚙️ Permanent Preferences & Rules

1. **Raw Storage Location:**
   Never migrate 100 GB+ of raw media into Obsidian or Cloudflare D1. Raw files stay in TeraBox, Telegram, Google Drive, Discord, or local storage. Second Brain stores only the compressed intelligence (2–10 KB) and a permanent pointer.

2. **Mobile Capture Speed:**
   Telegram is the primary mobile capture inbox on Android. Fast dumping of thoughts, screenshots, YouTube links, and voice notes takes priority over rigid upfront tagging.

3. **User vs AI Distinction:**
   Strictly distinguish my explicit thoughts (`my_thought`) from AI-generated insights (`insights`) and objective facts.

4. **Speed & Autonomy:**
   Ship products quickly and use AI to automate repetitive workflows.
