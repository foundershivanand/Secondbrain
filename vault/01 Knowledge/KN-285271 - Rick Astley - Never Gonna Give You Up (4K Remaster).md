# Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up (4K Remaster)

## Core Idea

The official 4K remastered music video for Rick Astley's 1987 hit 'Never Gonna Give You Up', which is the foundational medium for 'Rickrolling', one of the internet's most enduring bait-and-switch memes.

## Key Information

- Originally released in 1987 by British singer Rick Astley.
- The video is the source of the global internet phenomenon known as 'Rickrolling'.
- This specific version is a high-definition 4K remaster of the original music video.
- Rickrolling involves baiting a user into clicking a link that unexpectedly redirects them to this music video.
