# #0010 Video Knowledge Capture: Turning Short-Form Clips into Lasting Insights

> [!abstract] 💡 Fundamental Core Idea
> Short-form videos often contain dense educational value that gets lost without structured capture. This note outlines a workflow to extract, tag, and integrate video insights into a personal knowledge base for long-term retention.

> Hey everyone, today we're talking about video knowledge capture. Most people scroll past reels without saving what matters. Try it with your next educational clip.

- Transcribe the core idea immediately
- Tag the note to relevant projects, not just a general archive
- Prioritize insight extraction over link saving
