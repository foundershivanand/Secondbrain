# U Turn (Kannada Feature Film by Pawan Kumar)

## Core Idea

A mystery thriller centered on a journalism intern investigating fatal traffic violations on a Bangalore flyover, turning civic negligence into the premise for a supernatural thriller.

## Key Information

- Written and directed by Pawan Kumar, known for indie crowdfunding pioneer 'Lucia'.
- Stars Shraddha Srinath, Radhika Narayan, and Roger Narayan.
- Narrative premise: Commuters who illegally move concrete blocks on Bangalore's Double Road flyover to take an illegal U-turn end up dying mysteriously.
- Combines civic commentary on casual law-breaking and public apathy with psychological and supernatural investigative thriller elements.
