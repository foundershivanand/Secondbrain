# 12 Angry Men (1957)

## Core Idea

A classic legal drama film exploring jury deliberation, presumption of innocence, confirmation bias, and the power of critical thinking and persuasion in a murder trial.

## Key Information

- Depicts a 12-man jury deliberating the fate of an 18-year-old accused of patricide.
- Initially, 11 jurors vote 'guilty' based on superficial assumptions and personal biases, while one juror (Juror 8) votes 'not guilty' to prompt discussion.
- Systematically deconstructs witness testimony and physical evidence through logical reasoning and re-enactment.
- Highlights group dynamics, cognitive biases, impatience, and the psychological difficulty of standing alone against a consensus.
