## Core Idea

Amruthavarshini is a landmark 1997 Indian Kannada-language psychological romantic thriller film directed by Dinesh Baboo, renowned for its exploration of obsessive love, psychological tension, and an acclaimed musical score by Deva.

## Key Information

- Written and directed by Dinesh Baboo, starring Ramesh Aravind, Suhasini, and Sharath Babu in lead roles.
- The plot centers around a happily married couple whose lives are destabilized by an obsessive, psychopathic friend who falls in love with the wife.
