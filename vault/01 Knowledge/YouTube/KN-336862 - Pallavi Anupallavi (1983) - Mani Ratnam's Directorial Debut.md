# Pallavi Anupallavi (1983) - Mani Ratnam's Directorial Debut

## Core Idea

Pallavi Anupallavi (1983) is the landmark directorial debut of renowned Indian filmmaker Mani Ratnam, starring Anil Kapoor in his Kannada debut alongside Lakshmi, celebrated for its mature exploration of unconventional relationships and collaboration with Ilaiyaraaja and Balu Mahendra.

## Key Information

- Directed and written by Mani Ratnam, serving as his directorial debut in Indian cinema.
- Features Anil Kapoor in his sole Kannada-language film role, starring alongside Lakshmi and Kiran Vairale.
- Features an acclaimed technical crew including Balu Mahendra as cinematographer and Ilaiyaraaja as composer.
- The narrative centers around an unconventional romantic bond between a younger man and an older, separated woman, defying traditional Indian cinema tropes of the early 1980s.
