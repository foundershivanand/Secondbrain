# Cat On Chair: Gamified Focus and Anti-Doomscrolling App

## Core Idea

Using gamified virtual companions (cats) to incentivize focus sessions and block distracting apps, transforming digital detox into an emotionally rewarding experience.

## Key Information

- The app is called 'Cat On Chair'.
- It functions by blocking distracting apps while a focus timer is active.
- A virtual cat sits on a digital chair to keep the user company during the focus session.
- Users receive rewards (such as new cats or items) upon successfully completing their focus sessions.
- It is marketed as a tool to combat doomscrolling by making the interruption of focus annoying or emotionally disincentivized.

## Insights

- Kawaii (cute) UX design is a highly effective behavioral modification tool for younger demographics struggling with screen addiction.
- Shifting the framing of app blockers from punitive restriction ('You are locked out') to positive reinforcement ('Keep your cat company') improves user retention and emotional association with productivity.
