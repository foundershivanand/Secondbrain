# #0006 Framework for Identifying Core Personal Values

> [!abstract] 💡 Fundamental Core Idea
> Identifying your core values is essential to living in authentic alignment rather than defaulting to the conditioning and expectations of family, culture, and society. Clarifying key domains—such as achievement, family, service, justice, and health—provides an internal compass for intentional decision-making.

Identifying your top values is one of the most important things you can do to live in alignment with who you are. When we don't know our values, we live by default—absorbing what family, culture, and society tells us we should care about.

## 📑 Key Takeaways & Information
- Without conscious value identification, individuals live 'by default,' unconsciously absorbing external expectations from family and society.
- Key value archetypes illustrated: Achievements (striving, competence), Family (connection, lineage), Service (contribution, altruism), Justice (fairness, integrity), and Health (vitality, well-being).
- Self-definition through core values anchors authentic self-regulation, boundary-setting, and emotional safety.

## ⚛️ Atomic Knowledge Deductions
- **When an individual fails to define their personal values, their behavioral choices default to conditioning imposed by family, culture, and societal pressures.** *(Type: `PRINCIPLE` | Confidence: 96%)*
- **Core values serve as an internal decision-making rubric that aligns daily actions with intrinsic self-identity.** *(Type: `CONCEPT` | Confidence: 95%)*

## 🔭 Strategic Insights & Mental Models
- Default living is the psychological state of navigating life using borrowed moral and pragmatic frameworks rather than examined internal convictions.
- True alignment requires pruning competing values to isolate the top 3-5 non-negotiable principles governing daily choices.

## ✅ Actionable Implementation Items
- [ ] Audit major recurring life decisions against primary value categories (e.g., Health, Justice, Service, Family, Achievement) to identify value conflicts.
- [ ] Select top 3-5 core values and establish clear behavioral boundaries to protect them from external conditioning.
