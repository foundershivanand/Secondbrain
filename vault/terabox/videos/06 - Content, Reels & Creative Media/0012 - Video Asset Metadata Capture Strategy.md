# #0012 Video Asset Metadata Capture Strategy

> [!abstract] 💡 Fundamental Core Idea
> Effective knowledge management requires treating video assets as structured data rather than raw blobs. By systematically capturing metadata like timestamps and sources, you transform passive files into retrievable intellectual assets.

> [!quote] 🎙️ Reel Transcript & Audio Atmosphere
> **Audio Sound Profile:** Spoken direct-to-camera narrative with minimal lo-fi background beats
> 
> Hey everyone, today we're talking about video asset management. When you save a reel or clip, don't just store the file. Capture the metadata. Look at the filename, the timestamp, and the source. This turns a raw file into a searchable knowledge node. Use tools like ExifTool or manual tagging to record resolution, duration, and context. Now your library is a brain, not just a dump.

## 📑 Key Takeaways & Information
- Always extract filename and timestamp upon download
- Tag videos by content theme and source platform
- Record technical specs like resolution and duration
- Store metadata in a linked document alongside the file

## ⚛️ Atomic Knowledge Deductions
- **Metadata transforms unstructured media into queryable knowledge assets.** *(Type: `INSIGHT` | Confidence: 95%)*
- **Video files require contextual tags to remain useful over time.** *(Type: `CLAIM` | Confidence: 90%)*

## 🔭 Strategic Insights & Mental Models
- A video without metadata is lost knowledge; a video with metadata is a searchable node.
- Organizing by metadata schema scales better than organizing by folder hierarchy alone.
