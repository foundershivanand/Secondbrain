# Affidavit Format Template

## Core Idea

This document provides a standard format for drafting an affidavit, which is a sworn statement of facts used as evidence in legal proceedings. It outlines the necessary components and structure to ensure legal validity and admissibility.

## Key Information

- An affidavit is a formal written statement made under oath or affirmation.
- It must be signed by the deponent (the person making the statement) in the presence of an authorized officer.
- The document requires attestation by a person authorized to administer oaths, such as a Notary Public.
- A typical affidavit format includes a title, deponent's personal details, a clear statement of facts, and a jurat (attestation clause).
