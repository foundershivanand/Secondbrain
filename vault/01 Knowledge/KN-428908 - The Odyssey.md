## Core Idea

An epic recounting or adaptation of Homer's ancient Greek epic poem, The Odyssey, detailing Odysseus's arduous ten-year journey home to Ithaca following the Trojan War.

- Chronicles the trials and adventures of Odysseus returning from the Trojan War
- Encounters with mythological obstacles including the Cyclops, Circe, the Sirens, and Scylla
- The struggle of Penelope and Telemachus against suitors in Ithaca while awaiting Odysseus's return
