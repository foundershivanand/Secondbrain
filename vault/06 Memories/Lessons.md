# 💡 Lessons Learned

1. **Massive Context Windows Are Not Long-Term Memory:**
   Simply stuffing larger documents into LLM prompts leads to "needle in a haystack" loss, high latency, and massive cost. Hierarchical retrieval consistently outperforms raw context injection.

2. **Digital Hoarding vs Atomic Knowledge:**
   Saving hundreds of raw URLs and videos creates an illusion of learning. Without an automated extraction pipeline that converts media into atomic ideas, notes become an unsearchable digital landfill.

3. **Memory Needs Governance:**
   Blindly promoting every user conversation to permanent memory produces contradiction and noise. Daily consolidation with duplicate and conflict checking is essential.
