# Political Strategies of RSS and Muslim League During British Rule

## Core Idea

The RSS and the All-India Muslim League largely avoided mass anti-colonial movements during British rule, prioritizing communal consolidation and mutual competition over directly overthrowing the British Empire.

## Key Information

- The RSS and Muslim League focused on communal consolidation rather than mass anti-colonial resistance.
- The Muslim League, founded in 1906, sought to protect Muslim elite interests and leveraged separate electorates introduced via the Morley-Minto Reforms of 1909.
- During World War II, when the Indian National Congress launched the Quit India Movement in 1942, the Muslim League cooperated with the colonial administration to consolidate power and form provincial coalition governments.
