# 0009 • The Odyssey - Narrative Archetypes, Strategic Cunning, and the Hero's Return

> [!abstract] Core Idea
> Homer's epic narrative establishes the foundational Western archetype of the hero's journey, emphasizing intellect and strategic deceit over brute force. It explores themes of homecoming (nostos), temptation, hubris, and the restoration of civic and familial order against opportunistic exploiters.
