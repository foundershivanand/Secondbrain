# AI Workout Planner UI Concept by MiniMax M3

## Core Idea

A dynamic, context-aware AI fitness application concept that generates personalized workout sessions based on real-time equipment availability and user energy levels.

## Key Information

- The app builds custom workout sessions based on the specific gear the user has access to on any given day.
- Includes a subjective state check-in ('How are you feeling?') to tailor workout intensity.
- Features a clean three-screen flow: Equipment Selection, Workout Overview, and Active Exercise Tracking with built-in timers.
- Designed by MiniMax M3.
