# Beladingala Baale (1995 Kannada Film)

## Core Idea

A classic Kannada suspense-drama film directed by Sunil Kumar Desai, where a chess grandmaster is challenged to identify a mysterious female caller ('Beladingala Baale') who refuses to reveal her face or identity, creating a unique narrative of intellectual romance and suspense.

## Key Information

- Directed by Sunil Kumar Desai.
- Starring Anant Nag as Revanth, a chess grandmaster.
- The heroine's face is never revealed to the protagonist or the audience, maintaining a unique sense of mystery throughout the film.
- Based on a novel by Telugu writer Yandamoori Veerendranath.
