# Amruthavarshini (Kannada Film)

## Core Idea

Amruthavarshini is a landmark 1997 Indian Kannada-language romantic psychological thriller film written and directed by Dinesh Baboo, exploring themes of obsessive love, betrayal, and psychological manipulation.

## Key Information

- The film stars Ramesh Aravind, Suhasini Maniratnam, and Sharath Babu in principal roles.
- Directed and written by Dinesh Baboo, noted for its tight pacing, cinematography, and psychological tension.
- Features an acclaimed musical soundtrack composed by Deva, with iconic songs such as 'Tunturu' and 'Ee Gundu Pinna'.
- Ramesh Aravind plays an obsessive antagonist disguised as a devoted friend, marking a defining performance in Kannada cinema.
